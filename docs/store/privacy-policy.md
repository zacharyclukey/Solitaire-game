# Privacy Policy — Facedown

**Last updated: 2 September 2026**

Facedown does not collect any data about you.

## What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no
crash reporting and no third-party SDKs that gather information.

## What the app stores

Facedown saves your game on the device it is installed on: the run you are
part-way through, your records and statistics, and your settings. This is kept
in the app's own local storage. It never leaves your device, and we cannot see
it.

Deleting the app deletes it. You can also erase it from inside the app at any
time: **Settings → Erase all progress**.

## Network

Facedown makes no network requests while you play. The game and all of its
assets are bundled inside the app, and it is fully playable in aeroplane mode.

## Children

Because the app collects no data at all, it collects no data from children. It
contains no advertising, no links out of the app and no purchases.

## Changes

If a future version of Facedown ever collects anything, this policy will be
updated before that version ships, and the change will be described in the
release notes.
